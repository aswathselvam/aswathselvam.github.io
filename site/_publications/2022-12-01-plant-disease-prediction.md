A smart-agriculture platform where farmers get help with plant diseases from the community. Deep learning identifies the disease from a photo of the affected plant, NLP ranks the solutions posted by the community on a Twitter-based message channel, and a concept-drift approach adapts the recommended solution to changing conditions.
