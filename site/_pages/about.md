Hello! I'm Aswath Muthuselvam, a software engineer at [Magic Leap](https://www.magicleap.com/), where I work on ground-truth data using advanced camera systems and motion-capture systems for applications like hand tracking, localization and mapping.

I graduated from the University of Maryland, College Park, with a Master of Engineering in Robotics, and worked with Prof. Dinesh Manocha at the [GAMMA lab](https://gamma.umd.edu/). Before that, I earned a Bachelor's in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Vellore Institute of Technology, and interned at Ericsson India Global Services on deep-learning-based disease detection in horticultural crops.
